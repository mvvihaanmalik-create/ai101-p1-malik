# Style Note

Treat this as a contemporary-art exhibition poster, not a generic AI illustration. Build one strong focal artwork with a clear internal logic, hard-edged flat shapes, bold asymmetry, large negative-space zones, and controlled repetition. Use the references for their visual principles—monumental massing, consistent rhythm, human scale, and gallery restraint—not to copy their content or typography.

The student may establish scale and the act of making or displaying the work, but the exhibited art should dominate. Exclude decorative clutter, plants, furniture, random symbols, literal robot/circuit imagery, and text.
