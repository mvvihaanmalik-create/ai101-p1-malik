# Intent statement

## What I am building

**Part A.** A Technologist agent that checks feasibility before I start a prototype.

**Extra Part A role: Pip.** A design-reference agent that checks sources for the project I select. It supports the evidence pass in Step 2; it does not choose the design or replace the Technologist's route check.

**Part B.** A vault agent that follows my five-step IdeaQuest workflow and keeps my decisions, records, and approval gates under my control.

## Why

I have spent hours building from an idea before checking the tools, device limits, or effort. I want the agent to find those risks early and make me choose what to do next.

## The Project 2 workflow my skill will encode

IdeaQuest: define the goal, check feasibility, build a small prototype, do a human check, then iterate or stop.

## What the agents must never decide

They must not approve a route or gate, change my scope or risk limits, or decide to publish. Those choices stay with me.

## How I will know it works

When key facts such as the target device or budget are missing, the agent says so, compares routes with evidence, proposes a small test, and waits for my choice.

For Pip, the result is a small set of relevant references with original links and checked dates. Unknown dates stay in Archive. I choose what to use.
