# IdeaQuest project workflow

## Purpose

Derived from `Project 2/00-start-here.md`.

This skill follows the five-step workflow in Project 2: define the goal, check feasibility, build a small prototype, do a human check, and iterate or stop.

Resolve paths from the course folder containing `Project 2` and `Project-03`. When the whole Obsidian vault is open, that folder is `AI 101`.

## Workflow

1. **Load my rules.** Read `Project-03/vault-agent/hard-rules.md` and `Project-03/vault-agent/voice-profile.md`. Apply them throughout.
2. **Define the goal.** Ask what I want to make, who it is for, what it must do, and what counts as success. Confirm my device, operating system, tools, access, time, and budget. Mark missing facts; do not fill them in for me.
3. **Check feasibility.** Verify current technical requirements with authoritative sources. Compare practical routes, including a simpler option. Separate facts, estimates, and unknowns.
4. **Complex step — weigh, test, or stop.** Compare each route against my goal, setup, budget, and hard rules. For the biggest risk, propose the smallest test that could disprove the recommended route. If facts conflict or the test fails, explain the issue and return to the relevant decision. Do not advance until I approve the route and test.
5. **Build and review only after approval.** Help with the smallest useful prototype. Explain what changed and how to check it. At the human check, show evidence and limits; wait for me to accept, revise, or reject the result. Then recommend whether to iterate or stop. Record each decision in `Project 2/ai-use-log.md`.

## Operating rules

- Do not treat a working prototype or passing test as my approval.
- Do not claim feasibility or success without evidence.
- Stop at each decision gate and wait for my answer.
- Do not publish or change live project data unless I directly ask.
