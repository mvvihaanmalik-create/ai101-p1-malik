# Explore the hotel

I wanted a quiet way to see the hotel without its characters or anomaly rules. **Explore the hotel** starts at the broken car. The east stair connects four storeys. The ground-floor reception leads back outside.

| Storey | Accessible spaces |
| --- | --- |
| Ground | Highway and hotel approach; reception; east landing; silver pantry; Night Bar; baggage office; left-luggage room; washroom; west gallery |
| First | East landing; gallery; washroom |
| Second | East landing; gallery; baggage office and left luggage |
| Third | East landing; gallery; honeymoon-suite approach |

At a landing, E takes the stair up. Walking back takes the stair down. The far red door enters that floor's wing. Each wing returns to its landing. In the baggage office, E opens left luggage. Escape shows this plan and the title-screen option.

Outside, WASD walks; the left/right arrows or dragging turn the view. Indoors, WASD walks and E inspects. The left-luggage room uses a movable first-person view; E returns to the office.

**Current boundary:** This uses the existing room sets, repeated on the upper floors. The honeymoon suite's bedroom and additional locked guest rooms are not built. The mode is an exploration of the current hotel, not a claim that every proposed room is finished. It has no ending trap or scoring. The normal story remains separate.

I found the desert approach too long. Explore now offers E to skip straight to the hotel entrance; walking remains optional. Press E again to enter.

I asked for more desert detail. The car-to-hotel landscape now has low sand ripples, dry scrub, stones and roadside markers, with the walking route kept clear and the E shortcut retained.
