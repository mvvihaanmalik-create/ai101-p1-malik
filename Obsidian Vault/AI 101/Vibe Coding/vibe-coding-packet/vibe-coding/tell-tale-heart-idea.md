# The Tell-Tale Heart: anomaly game

**Status:** Candidate under consideration. Story and mechanics below are suggestions, not finalized decisions.

An anomaly game inspired by Edgar Allan Poe's short story. The player is the narrator, sitting with investigators after hiding evidence beneath the floorboards. The room changes while a heartbeat grows louder, and the player must notice what is wrong while managing mounting panic.

**Proposed interaction:** Repeatedly inspect the room. Anomalies could appear in a randomized order: a breathing floorboard, a portrait's moving eye, a turned chair, a detached shadow, or an investigator tapping to the heartbeat. One possible rule is to look away when an anomaly appears and continue the conversation when the room is normal. An incorrect response could increase panic.

**Proposed endings:** The investigators leave, or the narrator's panic leads to confession. How the story concludes and whether the anomalies are imagined or supernatural remain undecided.

**Open decisions:** Controls, number and behavior of anomalies, randomization, player response, consequences of errors, story and ending, visual direction, user, and scope.

**Story reference:** [Edgar Allan Poe, Short Fiction](https://standardebooks.org/ebooks/edgar-allan-poe/short-fiction). Record the specific edition and permission for any material actually used.
