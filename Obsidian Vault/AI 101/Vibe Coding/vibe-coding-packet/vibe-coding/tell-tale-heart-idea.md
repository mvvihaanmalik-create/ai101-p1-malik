# The Tell-Tale Heart
I considered adapting a small part of Poe’s story rather than a whole book. I was interested in using anomalies around the narrator and investigators while the heartbeat grew louder.

I chose Hotel of Horror instead. The Poe version’s controls, panic mechanic and ending stayed at the idea stage.
