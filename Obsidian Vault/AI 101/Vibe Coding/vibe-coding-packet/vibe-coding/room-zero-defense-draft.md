# Why I removed the holy item
I originally considered stabbing, shooting or using a holy item to repel threats. I tried a crucifix, then questioned whether it fit the pagan horror direction I wanted from Twin Peaks and Hokum.

I chose to remove it. The player now notices the butler’s floating bell and retreats through the entrance. Contact resets progress to ground.

This keeps the danger tied to the same observation and turning-back mechanic as the rest of the game.
