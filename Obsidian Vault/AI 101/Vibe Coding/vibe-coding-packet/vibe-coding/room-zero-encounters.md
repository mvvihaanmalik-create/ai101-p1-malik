The player first gets a chance to see the ordinary landing. In this proposal, the normal landing has no people or animals; framed staff portraits and a service bell establish the hotel setting. On later visits, a character appearing on the landing is a visible anomaly. This gives the player a fair reason to turn back even if the character seems harmless. Only an encounter marked **threat** approaches and can be repelled with the holy item.

| Character | Proposed role | What the player notices | Response |

## How the encounters fit the loop

- A normal landing contains no person or animal in this proposed baseline; continue to progress.
- Each listed encounter occupies one anomalous passage. Turn back to progress; continuing resets progress as in the approved loop.
- The butler is the proposed active threat: the holy item repels him briefly so the player can retreat. Exact distance, timing, cooldown, what happens if he reaches the player, and how his encounter is signaled need to be decided.
- Each encounter can be chosen from a randomized pool. The exact pool size, frequency, and repetition rules are open.

The baseline, six encounters, and classifications are proposed. Their animations, presentation, and feasibility have not been tested. The ending and why these characters are in the hotel are still open.
