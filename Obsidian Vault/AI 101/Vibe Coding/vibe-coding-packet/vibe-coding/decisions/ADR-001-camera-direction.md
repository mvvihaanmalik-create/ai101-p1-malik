# ADR-001 · Fixed cameras
November 5–6, 2026

**Why:** I wanted the changing viewpoints of the first Resident Evil, with the guest visible in some shots and absent in close-ups.

**My decision:** I chose fixed landing, corridor and stair views, plus inspection shots. I keep the guest’s face concealed.

**Tradeoff:** The compositions give me control over the mood. Camera cuts still need to preserve clear movement.
