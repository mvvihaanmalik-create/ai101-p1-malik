# ADR-014 · Tie the luggage puzzle to the story

**Failure.** Cycle 2 Test 3 feedback said that choosing a suitcase by icon felt random.

**Decision.** Match the honeymoon-suite receipt to its case in the mirror. Give the receipt yesterday’s date and put the staff-stair key in the case.

**Consequence.** The puzzle makes the hotel’s repetition visible while giving the player a reason to claim the case. The developer walkthrough shows it solved after six sightings.
