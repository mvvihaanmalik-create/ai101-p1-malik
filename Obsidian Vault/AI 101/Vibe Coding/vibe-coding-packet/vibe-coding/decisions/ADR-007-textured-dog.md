# ADR-007 · Replace the dog
November 10, 2026

**Why:** In [Test 1](../build-record/tests/formal-cycle-1/T1-result.md), I thought the dog looked extremely fake. I suggested a free reusable model.

**My decision:** I asked Codex to replace it. The game now uses German Shepherd by 3dskillup.art under CC BY 4.0, adapted for the standing dog, seated shadow and ending.

**Tradeoff:** The dog keeps its existing clue: its shadow changes posture. The creator needs credit. [Source and adaptations](../sources/assets-and-tools.md).
