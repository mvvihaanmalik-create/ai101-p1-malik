# Give the porter two short scenes

**Why:** After [Test 4](../build-record/tests/formal-cycle-1/T4-result.md), Vihaan asked for a fixed character animation to tell more of the story. He chose a practical exchange: an apology for the broken lift and directions to the suite.

**Decision:** The guest walks to the counter. The porter gives directions. At luggage claim he recognizes the matched case: “I carried this case up yesterday.” He turns to the stair door and opens it. The player decides what that implies.

**Tradeoff:** The welcome takes about 14 seconds and can be skipped with E. The second scene uses the existing puzzle, with no extra objective. In Cycle 2, watch whether the directions land and whether the player notices the repeated case.
