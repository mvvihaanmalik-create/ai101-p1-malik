# ADR-011 · Give the story a short scene
November 10, 2026

**Why:** After [Test 4](../build-record/tests/formal-cycle-1/T4-result.md), I wanted a fixed character animation to give the plot context.

**My decision:** I chose a walk to reception, a welcome, an apology for the broken lift and directions upstairs. At luggage claim, the porter recognizes the case and says he carried it up yesterday.

**Tradeoff:** The welcome lasts about 14 seconds and E skips it. The second exchange uses the existing puzzle rather than adding an objective.
