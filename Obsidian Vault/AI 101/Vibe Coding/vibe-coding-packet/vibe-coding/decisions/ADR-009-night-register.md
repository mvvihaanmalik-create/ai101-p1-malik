# ADR-009: Teach the decision at the place it happens

- Date: November 10, 2026. Evidence: [Vihaan's Test 2 observation](../build-record/tests/formal-cycle-1/T2-result.md). He wanted more detail for someone unfamiliar with anomaly games. The escape and reset worked on v13.
- Decision: Show an ordinary tour before scoring. Use the entrance, far door and top/bottom step as landmarks. Escape opens the night register with a description of the **ordinary** subject. Its text is identical for ordinary and changed versions. The bell warning names the retreat control and destination; the player has 5.5 seconds before pursuit starts. A wrong choice or contact explains the return to ground.
- Tradeoff: A newcomer can look up the normal condition; a player who wants to solve it unaided can leave the register closed. The warning is explicit because the butler is the one timed encounter.
- Check in play: can a first-time player explain where to cross and what a reset means without coaching? No novice result has been recorded.
