# ADR-004 · The suite returns to reception
November 8, 2026

**Why:** I wanted an ending that felt more hostile and closer to Twin Peaks.

**My decision:** I chose to make the suite door return the player to reception. The greeting repeats, the room darkens, the butler’s bones glow and the surrounding cast walks toward the guest.

**Tradeoff:** The opening must establish the desk and greeting clearly. The final image holds until replay. [Story](../room-zero-story-draft.md).

**Story clarification, November 11:** I am playing a new guest on their first visit. The broken lift sends me the long way upstairs to my honeymoon suite. The suite returns me to reception and reveals that I am trapped. I chose Hotel California as inspiration for that ending. This replaces the earlier family-hotel backstory; it does not establish a previous stay.
