# Last Train Home
I liked a strange train where anomalies appear in a randomized order and the player avoids them. I considered a playful, surreal cat version as well as a more modern anime direction.

I kept it as a strong alternative, then chose Hotel of Horror. I did not finalize the train’s story or controls.
