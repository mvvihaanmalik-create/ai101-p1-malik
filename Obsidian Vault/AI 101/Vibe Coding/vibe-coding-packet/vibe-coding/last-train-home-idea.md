# Last Train Home

**Status:** Saved for consideration. The project idea has not been selected.

A stray cat rides a strange train filled with forgotten things. Many weird anomalies appear in a randomized order, and the player guides the cat past them. The tone combines playful surrealism with an unsettling edge and a modern anime-inspired visual direction.

**Game inspiration:** The Exit 8, especially its anomalies. The player response and how randomized encounters work in this game are still to be decided.

**Earlier story suggestion, still open:** A glowing fish leads the cat through a carriage of forgotten objects toward a child's drawing. At the last stop, the cat meets the child, now older. How the anomalies fit this story, how the player avoids them, and what happens when caught are undecided.

**Open decisions:** The complete story and ending, controls, number and specific kinds of anomalies, how encounters are randomized and avoided, win and loss conditions, audience, scope, and visual references.
