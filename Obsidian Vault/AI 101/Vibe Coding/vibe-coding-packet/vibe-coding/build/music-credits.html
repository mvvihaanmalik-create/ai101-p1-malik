Return to Hotel of Horror

Hotel of Horror credits

The trapped-hotel ending draws on the Eagles song Hotel California. This game is an independent student project.

The hotel and the final reception have separate scores.

Human characters

Guest, butler, porter, attendant and children use adapted MakeHuman Community core and system graphical assets, assembled with MPFB. Selected graphical assets: CC0 1.0. Pack credit: makehuman_system; asset headers credit Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius and Jonas Hauquier.

Hotel of Horror adaptations include proportions, faces, fitted clothing, materials, authored skeletal animation and web exports. The portraits, empty coat and porcelain likeness derive from these assets. The ending uses original skeletal geometry fitted to the butler and the receptionist. The two head props reuse the adapted child mesh and its skin and hair materials.

Art and development

Concept, art direction, gameplay decisions and story: Vihaan Malik. Implementation, model adaptation and procedural asset work: Codex, directed by Vihaan.

Hotel architecture, lighting and offline images were authored with Python scripts in headless Blender 4.3.2. Blender MCP was requested but its use is not confirmed in the project records. The highway, steam, palms and hotel exterior are project-authored Three.js scenery. The driver uses the guest rig. Live mirrors, characters and effects use Three.js. Earlier moodboards were AI-generated concept references.

Reception and Night Bar

“Dance of the Dream Man (Instrumental)” by Angelo Badalamenti, from Music from Twin Peaks. Recording supplied by the project creator for use in this game. Room filtering, level and looping are applied during playback.

The suite approach and ending

“Hotel California” performed by Eagles. Written by Don Felder, Don Henley and Glenn Frey. The project creator supplied the recording. The final verse accompanies the walk to the suite and the return to reception. The same recording continues through the end titles and fades over the final ten seconds.

Footsteps and doors

Footsteps, latch, hinge, cloche resonance, leather handling, fabric and metal clicks come from RPG sounds by Kenney Vleugels. The car door opening and closing use CarDoorSFX by looneybits. The slowing engine adapts Racing car engine sound loops by domasx2. All three creators released these sounds under CC0 1.0.

The desk bell, room tone and low notes are original Web Audio synthesis. The final impact layers two of those notes with Kenney’s metal-pot recording.
