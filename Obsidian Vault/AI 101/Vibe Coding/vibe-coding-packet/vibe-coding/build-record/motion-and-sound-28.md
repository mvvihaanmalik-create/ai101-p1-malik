# Driver, hands and room sound

I asked for a convincing exit and steady hands on props after watching v27. Codex converted the central-driving concept car into a two-seat targa, moved the steering assembly and pedals, and adapted a full-body seated turn to the guest. The inside door pull and outside push point each have their own hand contact.

The server passes the glass with the near hand. The bartender receives it with the other near hand. Both reach to the same glass path. The traveller grasps the case handle, and the ordinary butler’s bell follows a socket in his fingers.

The local checks sampled 484 exit frames and two full cycles of the ordinary and changed bar and baggage scenes. Driver knee angles stayed between 53° and 163°. Palm contact at the two measured door holds stayed within 1 mm. The server, bartender and traveller held within 27° of their sampled wrist poses. I can judge the performance in the [recorded close-ups](<tests/motion-sound-28/Motion and sound.mp4>).

The first sound pass gives the pantry a brighter reflection, the baggage office a shorter response, and the night bar the clearest piano. Cloth enters from alternating sides in the final reception. [Model and sound credits](../sources/assets-and-tools.md)
