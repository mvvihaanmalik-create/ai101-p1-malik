# Visual polish · Hotel of Horror

I wanted the characters to feel grounded in the hotel. Codex matched their lights to the lamps in each shot, fitted the guest’s leg pose to the stair treads and gave the receptionist a glance toward the guest. The porch lanterns light the entrance as the driver reaches the door.

The car dips under braking and settles after the tyre fails. Luggage cases keep their momentum when I reverse a turn. In the ending, the cast leaves the furniture behind. The guests approach with different strides, and the dog takes four-legged steps toward the player.

Codex checked the six main rooms, opening, luggage room, all twelve anomaly subjects and final reception in desktop Chrome. A separate keyboard run completed the orientation, four decisions, luggage claim, suite and ending. All 25 automated tests passed. This was a developer check.
