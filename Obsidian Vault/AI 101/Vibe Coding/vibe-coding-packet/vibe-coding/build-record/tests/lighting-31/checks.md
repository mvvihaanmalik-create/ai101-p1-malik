# Lighting and bar hands · v31

I wanted a scarier hotel with sharper materials. The gallery now catches cold light from its divided windows, and a thin line of light falls from the suite threshold. The washroom has veined stone, recessed grout and a ceramic glaze. Codex graded 37 original background renders and matched their smaller loading images. [Before and after](comparison.jpg)

I spotted the two hands merging in the Night Bar. The server now sets the glass down and withdraws. The bartender waits before picking it up. When the room changes, the glass rises to his waiting hand. [Server lifts](after/server-lifts.jpg) · [Glass down](after/glass-down.jpg) · [Bartender lifts](after/bartender-lifts.jpg)

Codex checked the projected hand geometry in both cameras at 60 samples per second during ordinary service, the changed version and the wine request. On a 1280-pixel picture, the narrowest gap was 40 pixels in the wide shot and 101 pixels at the counter. All 30 automated checks passed.

The browser check covered the road, all nine walkable hotel rooms, both washroom reflections, the luggage puzzle and four shots of the ending. [Browser result](after/browser-lighting-result.json)
