# Cycle 2, Test 1 · Main path and ending
November 12, 2026 · My playtest and follow-up feedback, with revisions through v35

I played the hotel and grouped my last ten prompts into this test. Walking felt slow, I had trouble spotting anomalies, and the two characters’ hands merged at the bar. I shared a screenshot of their hands.

| What I wanted to change | What I asked for |
| --- | --- |
| The ordinary tour and the climb | Faster walking while holding a key, friendlier faces and written greetings on the first visit, then stranger behavior upstairs. Clearer anomalies and more natural movement. |
| The rooms and exterior | A clean or bloodied reflection in its own bathroom, a wine exchange in the bar, four storeys outside, eerier lighting and sharper surfaces. |
| The suite reveal | Start my song on the approach after four correct decisions. Time the lyrics and fixed shots to it. Make the faces and camera angles more unsettling. |
| The credits | Carry the song into the title. Lead with my name, then credit the music, inspirations and sources and include the disclaimer. Keep the credits under 90 seconds, thank David Lynch and wish him rest in peace, then briefly thank Professor Nathan Madrid. Fade the last ten seconds to silence. |

**Comparison with Cycle 1: inconclusive overall.** Hands still broke the illusion in both tests. This time I could point to the exact overlap at the counter. I didn’t give a new verdict on the dog or wall lettering. My notes cover several revisions and don’t establish every route checkpoint on one build.

**What Codex verified afterward.** The bar handoff had a visible gap in the checked views. The revised title and credits ran 84 seconds, including six seconds for David Lynch and four for Professor Madrid. The ending recording captures the suite approach through replay becoming available.
