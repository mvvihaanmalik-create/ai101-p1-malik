# Cycle 2, Test 3 · Controls and character believability
November 13, 2026 · Public live build, expected v36 (version not independently checked during this attempt)

**Status: incomplete.** I watched the game in the in-app browser while Codex operated it. I gave the feedback below. The recording did not start successfully, and repeated graphics failures interrupted the control test. No usable video was saved. These are requests, not verified fixes.

## My feedback

- **Bar:** The cup grip still needs work. I want a more exaggerated, frightening bartender anomaly.
- **Porter:** The luggage interaction feels awkward, and his movement is stiff and unnatural.
- **Suitcase puzzle:** Choosing my icon feels random and confusing. I want the choice to mean something in the story.
- **Mirror:** I want my character to resemble Agent Dale Cooper / Kyle MacLachlan as closely as possible, especially in the Twin Peaks-inspired mirror scene.
- **Butler:** His bell should make a ringing sound.
- **Reception:** The characters look like floating half-bodies behind the counter. Their bodies and framing need to feel grounded.
- **Opening, follow-up feedback:** The receptionist's text disappears too quickly. I want a longer fixed opening with the protagonist driving, the car failing, a scratched-out hotel sign and dark, cinematic black-and-white highway shots. The car exit needs more polish. Another opening puzzle is an idea to consider, not a decided addition.

## What actually happened
