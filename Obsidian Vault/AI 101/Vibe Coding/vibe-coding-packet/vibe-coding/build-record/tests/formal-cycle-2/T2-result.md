# Cycle 2, Test 2 · Character movement and the reception ending
November 12, 2026 · v35 → v36

**What I noticed.** Glitchy movement and bodies blending together broke the illusion, especially when everyone approached me at reception. I wanted to see each character’s face, hands and task clearly while they walked.

**What I changed.** I asked for smoother movement throughout the hotel and separate paths for the final approach. The woman who checked me in stays expressionless as she flickers between human and skeleton. Beside her, the bald butler grins, rings his bell and flickers too. I wanted eccentric smiles and dilated pupils on the approaching cast. Closer, sharper shots show the kid dribbling a head, blood dripping from the dog’s mouth, the bartender pouring wine down his jacket and the attendant lifting the dish cover to reveal another head.
