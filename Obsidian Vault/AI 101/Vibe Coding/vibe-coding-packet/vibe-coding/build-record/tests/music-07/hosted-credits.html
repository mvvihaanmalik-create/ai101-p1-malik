The music of Room 0

Two independently released tracks accompany the hotel and its final reception. These are not recordings from Twin Peaks.

Hotel: Night on the Docks – Piano

“Night on the Docks – Piano” by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com).
Original track · Creative Commons Attribution 4.0.

Adapted for the game: level normalization, MP3 compression, edge fades, looping and a soft low-pass filter during playback.

Ending: Long Note Four

“Long Note Four” by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com).
Original track · Creative Commons Attribution 4.0.

Adapted for the game: a 32-second opening excerpt, level normalization, MP3 compression, fades and a volume envelope matched to the 26-second ending.

Music used under CC BY 4.0. No endorsement by the composer is implied. Footsteps, bells, room ambience and threat sounds are the game's original synthesis.
