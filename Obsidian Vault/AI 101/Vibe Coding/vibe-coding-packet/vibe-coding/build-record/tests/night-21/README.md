# The night arrival
I wanted the car to hold up in close-up and the hotel to appear through silhouettes and red windows. I kept the game title **Hotel of Horror**. On the building, only **HOTEL** survives beside the scratched paint.

The guest's car brakes and settles onto its flat tyre. He turns in the seat, clears the sill, plants his feet and braces as he stands. After closing the car door, he sees the windows, crosses the approach and presses the entrance lever. The hotel door swings inward and he walks through the opening into reception.

The exterior lasts about 28 seconds in game time. E skips it and Escape pauses. The car is the licensed Car Concept asset, adapted with wheel, tyre, suspension and door animation. The guest uses the existing 53-bone MakeHuman rig with hand and foot targets authored for this scene. These changes run in Three.js.
