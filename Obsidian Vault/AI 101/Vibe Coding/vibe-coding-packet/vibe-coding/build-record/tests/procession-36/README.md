# Reception procession

I wanted the receptionist to stay expressionless while she and the bell-ringing butler slip between flesh and bone. The others grin as they approach. The kid dribbles a head, the dog bleeds from the mouth, the bartender pours wine down his jacket, and the room-service attendant uncovers another head.

I kept the approach wide enough to show their separate paths, then used close shots to show what each person carries. The movement pass also smooths direction changes and gives the guest space around the ordinary cast.

Codex checked the ordinary tour, the luggage puzzle, four scored decisions, the suite and replay. The finale checks cover each close-up. These are developer checks.
