# Test 3 follow-up · The luggage guest
November 14, 2026

I could not read the luggage guest's face or tell which way he stood. He also looked too similar to the bartender. I asked for a thin, much older man with longer grey hair, a larger beard and glasses.

I changed his appearance across the luggage puzzle, ordinary and altered mirror encounter, and reception ending. The cases no longer block his face in the puzzle view. A small face light makes his features readable. His head now returns to its sampled pose before each glance; the old additive motion could accumulate because constant animation tracks skipped repeated writes.

**Checks:** All 31 automated checks passed on the final design. The luggage puzzle completed, and a twelve-second pose check stayed below 25 degrees of added head rotation. Graphics recovery, hotel audio and both mirror versions passed in the browser. All nine ending camera views passed after the longer hair and beard revision. The ending also completed through its credits. These are developer checks, not my finished observed Test 3.

The first ending check reached the credits but timed out: its 55-second wait was shorter than the credits. The test wait was corrected. No game failure is claimed from that timeout.
