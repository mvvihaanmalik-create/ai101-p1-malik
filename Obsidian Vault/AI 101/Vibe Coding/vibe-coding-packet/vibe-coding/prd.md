# Hotel of Horror

**Player.** I built it for friends who like atmospheric horror and games about noticing small changes.

**Goal.** As a first-time guest stranded in the desert, reach the honeymoon suite. The lift is broken, so the route goes up the stairs. The suite reveals that the hotel is a trap.

**Play.** The first visit teaches the ordinary rooms. Then I follow the night register, inspect one change per visit and turn back to log it. Three sightings advance a floor. A missed change or contact with the butler repeats the visit. After six sightings I use a mirror to match a luggage receipt to a case and open the staff stair. Twelve sightings open the final route.

**Scope.** Desktop keyboard. One hotel reuses room sets across four floors. One luggage puzzle and one ending. Optional staff conversations and empty-hotel exploration sit outside the scoring loop.

## Done when

- The ordinary tour teaches the rooms. A complete ascent logs twelve distinct changes over four floors.
- Camera cuts preserve control. Clues stay readable and pause and replay work.
- The bell warns me in time to retreat. Butler contact repeats the encounter without erasing recorded sightings.
