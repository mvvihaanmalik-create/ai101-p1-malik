# Hotel of Horror
**My goal:** Make a polished horror game that my friends and I would enjoy, built around noticing something wrong in an unsettling hotel.

**Player goal:** As a new guest visiting for the first time, reach my room, the honeymoon suite. The broken lift forces me to take the long way upstairs. Reaching the suite reveals that the hotel is a loop and I am trapped.

## The experience
1. **Learn and observe.** After a flat tyre strands me on a California desert highway, I walk to a hotel with red-lit windows. The receptionist turns toward the east wing. A lift notice directs me upstairs. The four-storey exterior leads into an ordinary first visit. The staff greet me in writing. I can ask for wine in the bar and inspect my face in the washroom mirror. Every scored visit changes. I spread encounters across the stairs, gallery and lift landing so each has room to breathe.
2. **Choose and retreat.** I follow the register to the affected part of the hotel, inspect the change and turn back. The hotel keeps each sighting. A missed change or contact with the butler repeats that visit.
3. **Claim and finish.** At 2/4, match a suitcase to its stamp in the mirror. The stamped ticket says I claimed it yesterday. The porter opens the staff stair. The suite returns me to reception. A bell rings, the butler’s skin gives way to bone, and the other guests stop around me. His face cuts into view just before everything goes black.

**Look and feel:** I chose Twin Peaks and Hokum for the atmosphere, Exit 8 for observation and Resident Evil for fixed cameras. Warm lamps, red curtains, dark wood and cold window light follow [Visual Study 02](sources/moodboards/room-zero-visual-study-02.md).

**Controls:** WASD/arrows walk; holding a direction builds speed. E inspects or uses a door; Escape pauses and opens the night register.

**Scope:** Connected hotel rooms reused across the ascent. No combat, mobile controls, additional endings or separate architecture for every floor.

**Optional exploration:** I added an empty-hotel mode from the title screen. It starts at the car, connects four storeys using the existing room sets, and plays quieter, muffled music. It has no people, anomaly rules, score or ending trap. The [layout](hotel-exploration-plan.md) states which rooms exist and which remain unbuilt.

## Done when
- I want every completed story to show all twelve changes. I inspect one change per scored visit and return to record it. Three sightings advance a floor. A missed change repeats.
- Clues stay readable, camera cuts preserve control, and pause and replay work.
- The butler gives a warning, allows retreat and restarts the current encounter on contact.

I can talk to the receptionist, bartender and luggage-room porter during the story. T opens a silent conversation. Staff remember recent exchanges with me and can refer to the changes I witnessed. Talking is optional.
