# Desert rover exploration

**Status:** Strong candidate, saved for consideration. The project idea has not been selected.

A rover explores a surreal alien desert, inspired by space-rover exploration, David Lynch's Dune, and surreal art. The player encounters anomalies to avoid and different types of aliens that chase the rover; shooting is a possible response. The effects of shooting, controls, enemy types, and scope remain open.

**Proposed story, still open:** The rover follows a signal to retrieve a missing probe. Shadows and tracks behave strangely on the way. At a distant doorway, the rover finds the probe and a recording revealing an enormous eye on the horizon. It retrieves the recording and returns to the lander; the eye closes as it leaves.

**Proposed encounters, still open:** An alien follows the moving rover, a shifting area affects steering, and a rushing attacker can be driven away by a pulse. A smaller first version could use one alien and one anomaly.

**Reference:** [Lunar rover post on X](https://x.com/hakimieiqbal/status/2088906801748062526). Post text was accessible; video could not be played. Its claims were not independently verified.

**Open decisions:** Story and ending, player goal, controls, shooting effects, anomalies, alien behaviors, audience, visual direction, and scope.
