# Room 0

**Chosen ending, November 8:** Opening the honeymoon suite returns the player to reception. The butler repeats the opening greeting, the view blurs and the room darkens. His flesh becomes invisible, exposing glowing skull and bones. Looking behind reveals the glowing anomaly figures surrounding the player and walking toward them. The game ends here. See room-zero-story-draft.md and decisions/ADR-004-reception-ending.md.

**Approved visual reference:** Visual Study 02 was selected on November 5: warm amber lamps, cold window beams, rich shadows, walnut, oxblood, olive and brass, with more uncanny hotel characters. See sources/moodboards/room-zero-visual-study-02.md. Camera framing, sound and implementation remain open.

**Direction:** Reach the haunted honeymoon suite while avoiding anomalies. The lift is broken, so the preferred route is the stairs; the hotel's halls are a fallback if stairs are too difficult to make. Views vary, inspired by the first Resident Evil: sometimes the character is visible and sometimes not. The face stays hidden; visible clothing includes a shirt, tie, and blazer. The aesthetic mixes the 2026 film Hokum and Twin Peaks. Exact framing and camera changes remain open.

**Gameplay direction:** In the spirit of The Exit 8, the player notices anomalies and turns back. Vihaan approved a loop in which a holy item briefly repels an approaching threat, giving the player time to turn back; stabbing and shooting were earlier alternatives. See room-zero-gameplay-proposal.md for the approved loop and details still open.

**Earlier story suggestion, superseded by the new destination and broken-lift premise:** A guest arrives at an almost empty hotel during a storm and finds only one door on the assigned floor: Room 0. A voice on the phone says the elevator will take the guest home once the room is ready. Each trip through the door returns the player to the same room, where something may have changed.

**Earlier proposed interaction, not approved:** Inspect a repeating room and stay when an anomaly appears or leave when it is normal. The current direction is to avoid anomalies on the way to the suite; the exact rules are open.

**Possible anomalies:** Rain falls upward, a second bed appears only in the mirror, a telephone cord moves, a suitcase breathes, a room-service tray contains a miniature room, or a person stands outside the sixth-floor window.

**Possible characters and encounters:** A weird, scary butler approaches the guest, inspired by the butler in Twin Peaks; creepy room service; children playing and looking for their ball; an abandoned dog; a server and bartender; and a person with luggage. See room-zero-encounters.md for a proposed cast and classifications awaiting review.

**Earlier proposed story arc, not selected:** Phone calls reveal why the room is trying to keep the guest there, and the elevator eventually opens. What happens at the honeymoon suite and the ending are undecided.

**Open decisions:** Stairs or halls based on feasibility; exact camera angles and switching; controls, anomaly count and randomization; proposed encounter classifications and threat behavior, holy item details and consequences; story and ending, user, visual details, and scope.
