# ADR-003: Anomalies and defense

**Status:** Gameplay loop approved by Vihaan on November 5; implementation details pending.

**Context:** The guest needs a way to encounter anomalies and threats on the route to the honeymoon suite.

**Decision:** The player continues on a normal passage and turns back on an anomaly, gaining progress for a correct choice and resetting progress on a mistake. Four correct steps lead to the suite. A holy item briefly repels approaching threats and has a cooldown. The loop draws on The Exit 8 and has some R.E.P.O. inspiration. Exact item, threat behavior, and cooldown remain open.

**Consequence:** The game can combine observation with more active encounters. Both systems need clear rules so players understand when to turn back and when a defensive action applies; their scope affects the time available for polish.

**November 6 decision:** Vihaan approved normal and anomalous character variants, twelve anomaly ideas and the butler as the first active threat; see ../room-zero-encounter-pool-v2.md.

**November 8 decision:** Vihaan approved the tarnished brass crucifix and contact with the butler resetting progress to zero and returning the guest to the landing. Initial durations remain tuning values; see ../room-zero-defense-draft.md.
