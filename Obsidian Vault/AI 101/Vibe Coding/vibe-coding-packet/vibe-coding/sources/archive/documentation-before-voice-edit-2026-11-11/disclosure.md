# Room 0 · AI and asset disclosure
Current through v18, November 10, 2026.

**Vihaan's contribution:** Chose Room 0, the hotel and honeymoon-suite setting, Exit 8 observation mechanic, fixed cameras, aesthetic references, characters and hostile reception ending. His playtest feedback directed clearer guidance, separated encounters, stairs, the luggage puzzle, porter dialogue and visual revisions.

**Codex's contribution:** Drafted project documents from those directions; wrote gameplay, interface and audio code; adapted models; authored animation and procedural geometry; ran Blender scripts, renders and exports; integrated assets; performed developer checks and deployed the game. Visual Studies 01 and 02 were AI-generated concept images.

**Reused work:** The humans, clothing, skin and facial components derive from MakeHuman CC0 assets, assembled using MPFB. The dog is German Shepherd by 3dskillup.art under CC BY 4.0. Kevin MacLeod composed the two licensed music tracks. Fonts and Three.js are external dependencies. [Creators, licences, modifications and evidence](sources/assets-and-tools.md).

**Blender workflow:** Blender 4.3.2 ran locally in background mode through Python scripts. Blender MCP was requested but unavailable in the documented initial session; no confirmed MCP execution is recorded for the subsequent passes. Add-on startup messages are not MCP call evidence. [Tasks and scripts](sources/assets-and-tools.md#blender-and-blender-mcp).

**Testing:** Codex operated the controls during the recorded Cycle 1 work; Vihaan supplied observations. Developer checks establish specific behaviour, not independent novice feedback or a human realism judgment. [Actual formal results](build-record/testing-and-failure-log.md). Cycle 2 has a test card; no formal Cycle 2 results have been recorded.

[Detailed disclosure history](disclosure-full-record.md) preserves the earlier per-build statements. They describe their original builds, including the superseded procedural dog and removed crucifix.
