# ADR-001: Changing views

**Status:** Camera direction chosen November 5; route and controls accepted by Vihaan November 6, 2026. Exact placement and movement tuning pending.

**Context:** Room 0 needs a visual perspective for navigating an unsettling hotel toward the honeymoon suite.

**Decision:** Use changing views inspired by the first Resident Evil: landing, corridor and stair-approach fixed views, plus object close-ups. Switch main views at defined spatial boundaries. Sometimes the guest is visible, sometimes not; the guest's face stays hidden, and the clothing specified is a shirt, tie, and blazer. Use desktop WASD/arrows to walk, E to inspect, F for the holy item and Escape to pause. Exact shot positions and movement feel will be checked in the prototype.

**Consequence:** Different views can shape what the player sees, but transitions and character visibility will need careful design to keep the room understandable.

**November 6 approval:** See ../room-zero-route-and-controls.md for the approved landing, corridor and short stair route, three main views, object close-ups and controls.
