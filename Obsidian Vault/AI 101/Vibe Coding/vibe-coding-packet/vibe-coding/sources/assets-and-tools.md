## Blender and Blender MCP
Blender MCP was requested for the initial prototype. The [technical record](../room-zero-technical-approach.md) states that its tools were unavailable. The documented modelling, rigging, exports and offline rendering used **Blender 4.3.2 in background mode, driven by Codex-authored Python scripts**.

The BlenderMCP add-on registered during some launches. That startup message does not establish an MCP tool call. No confirmed Blender MCP execution is recorded for these passes, so they are credited as headless Blender work.

## The roadside car · v21
I chose a detailed articulated vehicle for the driver's exit. Unity Fan created the original Concept Car 004 and released it under CC0. Eric Chadwick prepared the model and textures for Car Concept, © 2024 Darmstadt Graphics Group GmbH, under CC BY 4.0. The source file also identifies the Khronos and 3D Commerce marks as Khronos trademarks. [Upstream model record](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/CarConcept) · [Original Unity Fan model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/free-concept-car-004-public-domain-cc0-4cba124633eb494eadc3bb0c4660ad7e) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) · [Retained upstream credits](assets/car-concept/upstream.md).

The source supplies the cabin, wheels, textures and opening door hierarchy. Codex added wheel and suspension controls, a deforming flat tyre, night materials, cabin lighting and door timing. The driver uses the existing MakeHuman rig. His seated turn, foot planting, bracing, latch contact and walk through the entrance use original animation code in Three.js. The revised night sequence was authored in the game engine. [Car rig](../build/src/night-car.js) · [Driver movement](../build/src/road-driver.js).

Source GLB SHA-256: c272098089d78c5cd9fd9f24ff50ee8acf8dc932c55f2d55fc10adb6c8998966b.

I supplied the receptionist’s portrait reference for v22. The adapted human, long01 hair foundation, skin, eyes, teeth and fitted uniform come from the retained MakeHuman CC0 pack. Codex sculpted the face, styled the hair and authored the swept wave sections, eyeliner and hoops in headless Blender. [Character record](room-zero-character-assets.md).

The [sound credits](sound-credits.md) list the music, source recordings and synthesized cues in the current build.

## Arrival and hotel sounds · v23

I wanted the flat tyre and the walk from the car to feel like one continuous arrival. The engine winds down as the car stops. I used separate recordings for opening and closing the car door. At the hotel, a metal latch clicks just before the hinge creaks.

| Sound in my game | Creator and source | What changed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Engine | domasx2 · [Racing car engine sound loops](https://opengameart.org/content/racing-car-engine-sound-loops) | The source loops a public-domain car recording. Codex pitched it down over the stop and faded the tail. |
| Car door | looneybits · [CarDoorSFX](https://opengameart.org/content/cardoorsfx) | Codex trimmed and timed both recordings to the animated door. |
| Shoes, hotel latch and hinge | Kenney Vleugels · [50 RPG sound effects](https://opengameart.org/content/50-rpg-sound-effects) | Three shoe strikes follow the walking cycle. They sound softer on carpet and sharper on the stairs. |

All three packs use [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). I kept the [source downloads and licence evidence](audio-23/) and a [short sound preview](../build-record/tests/face-sound-23/foley-preview.mp3).

## Driver performance and room sound · v28

I wanted the driver to rise with his weight over his foot and touch the door as it moved. Codex adapted Microsoft Rocketbox’s seated left turn to my guest rig and the two-seat cockpit. The movement source is a chair take. The sill clearance, inside pull, exterior push and door timing are game adaptations. [Original take, copyright 2020 Microsoft](https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox/blob/0943055db6ec570bcef9f2c8b41c9e5467c808f9/Assets/Animations/all_animations_max_motextr_xyz/m_sit_stand_up_chair_left.max.fbx) · [MIT notice](../build/assets/rocketbox-LICENSE.txt)

I used four more sounds from Kenney Vleugels’ CC0 RPG pack. A pot strike voices the cloche, a metal click lands the glass and case clasp, handled leather accompanies the luggage puzzle, and cloth movement marks the car exit and approaches in the ending. The motor, airflow, panning and short room reflections are original Web Audio work. [Kenney’s pack](https://opengameart.org/content/50-rpg-sound-effects) · [Retained licence](audio-23/kenney-license.txt)

## Lighting and surfaces, v31

I wanted the hotel to feel scarier while keeping the clues readable. Codex graded the original Blender camera renders at their native resolution, adjusted the live stage lights and made the washroom stone, walnut and tile relief in code. This pass reused the credited character and vehicle models.

## Reception procession, v36

I used the credited MakeHuman cast and German Shepherd for the ending. The heads on the tray and stair landing share the child mesh. The finale restores skin and hair on the two head props. Codex animated the handbell, wine stream, muzzle drips and skeleton changes in Three.js.
