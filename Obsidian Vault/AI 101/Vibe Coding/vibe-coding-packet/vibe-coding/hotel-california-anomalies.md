# Hotel California and the washroom mirror
I chose the song for the feeling of checking into a place I can never leave.

In the Night Bar, I can ask for wine. The bartender glances at his bottle, opens his hand toward it and shakes his head. His answer appears in writing. I wanted the reference to happen once so the glass handoff stays readable on later visits.

I drew the bathroom mirror from Twin Peaks. I see myself in the glass on the ordinary tour. In the changed room blood covers my reflected face and runs onto my suit. I turn back. I replaced the old gallery mirror to keep twelve anomalies.

## Further proposals
I am still considering these ideas.

| Motif | Ordinary | Possible anomaly |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Checkout without departure | A guest’s entry in a register says they are staying. | The entry is stamped DEPARTED, while that guest stands beside the desk. The stamped departure keeps moving further into the past. A large inspectable page makes the contradiction readable. |
| The feast and the unkillable creature | Staff cut and serve a meal in a small dining-room glimpse. | Two staff repeatedly stab a covered shape. It keeps breathing and pulling the cloth inward. The player watches from the doorway. |
| The night attendant | A night porter gives directions in a low, human voice. | He repeats his welcome with exactly the same movement and timing. A second voice begins speaking from his chest while his mouth stays closed. |


The final return to reception remains the story’s full reveal of the trap. A random encounter should hint at confinement without staging the complete ending early.
