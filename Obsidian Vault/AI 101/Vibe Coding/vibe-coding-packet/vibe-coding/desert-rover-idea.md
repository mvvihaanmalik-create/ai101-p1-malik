# Desert rover
I considered a rover exploring a surreal alien desert, drawing on Dune, David Lynch and surrealist art. I wanted anomalies to avoid and different aliens that could chase the rover, with shooting as a possible response.

I kept this as an alternative and chose Hotel of Horror for the project. The rover’s story and combat were never finalized.
