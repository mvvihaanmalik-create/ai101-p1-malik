# Why I chose Hotel of Horror
I wanted to build a place that felt beautiful, strange and threatening. A hotel gave me a clear destination: the honeymoon suite. I chose a new guest visiting for the first time. The broken lift forces them to take the long way upstairs, only to discover that the hotel is a loop and a trap. Hotel California inspired that ending.

I liked Exit 8’s rule of noticing changes and turning back. I wanted that mechanic in a hotel shaped by Twin Peaks and Hokum, with fixed views inspired by Resident Evil.

I chose one complete storyline with detailed polish over adapting a whole book or building a large game. The current game has twelve possible anomalies, a short luggage puzzle and a hostile reception ending.
