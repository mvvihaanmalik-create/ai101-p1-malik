# My plan
1. **Idea:** I’m making Hotel of Horror, a browser horror game.
2. **Who it’s for:** My friends Tiffany, Katie, Aleela, Marie and Ornilla and I love Exit 8 and strange horror. I want to give them a finished game to play.
3. **Build:** When a player inspects an anomaly and turns back, the hotel logs the sighting. Three sightings advance a floor. Twelve open the route to the honeymoon suite.
4. **Boundary:** One hotel, four floors, twelve anomalies, one luggage puzzle and one ending. I chose to finish one story in two weeks.
5. **Rejected idea:** I considered adapting Poe’s *The Tell-Tale Heart*. I chose the hotel because one setting let me turn a repeating route into the story itself. [How the original idea changed](room-zero-story-draft.md).
