# ADR-013 · Make every anomaly part of the ascent

**Failure.** Four decisions could end the story before players saw most of the twelve encounters. Some clues also went unnoticed.

**Decision.** Give players an unscored tour, then require all twelve changes in shuffled order. Inspection and retreat log a sighting. Three sightings advance a floor, and a missed change repeats the visit.

**Consequence.** The finished route shows the whole cast and clue set. Runs are longer. Codex checked 200 shuffled routes and all twelve inspection views.
