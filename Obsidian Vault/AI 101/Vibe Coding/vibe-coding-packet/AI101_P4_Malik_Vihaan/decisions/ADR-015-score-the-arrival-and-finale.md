# ADR-015 · Use the supplied music to time the film

**Failure.** The arrival felt too short and the final reveal needed clearer staging, closer shots and shorter credits.

**Decision.** Use the car radio to lead into the 59-second opening and time the approach to the suite, the door reveal, the cast and the credits to the supplied recording. Preload demanding shots and give each performer room in the finale.

**Consequence.** Music and titles share a clock with the cinematics. Browser recovery and timing checks supplemented the formal tests. The GitHub copy includes sound effects and omits the supplied commercial recordings.
