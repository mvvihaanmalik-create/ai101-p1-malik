# ADR-012 · Make hands and movement believable

**Failure.** Cycle 1 Test 1 exposed unnatural hands. In Cycle 2, hands overlapped at the bar and bodies merged in the final approach.

**Decision.** Keep hands on their own side of the body, keep contact with the glass and candle, separate the finale paths, and turn the guest before walking back.

**Consequence.** The staging reads more clearly. Grip clearance and pose continuity passed developer checks. The interrupted player rerun remains open.
