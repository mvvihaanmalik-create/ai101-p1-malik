I start a project often not knowing the technical requirements to build it and it ends up looking different/worse from what I expected. How can I know which tools will I be needing for the specific project? How can I know about alternative methods/routes/outcomes? How can I know OS specific constraints and workarounds?

STEPS:
1. I start thinking about an idea in my head
2. I start searching for existing projects and take further inspiration to add a feature or functionality to my project idea.
3. The LLM agrees/approves and I start prototyping an initial concept for a few hours.
4. I have full faith until I've spent lots of tokens and hours and the outcome is just technically not feasible with the tools/resources being used or the time duration or tokens required etc.
5. I ditch the idea fully or partially and start research again and start over with a redefined goal and functionality that I find more achievable than the previous, rather difficult idea.

QUESTION?
How can LLMs effectively help non-technical people code?
How can non technical people use LLMs to code without being misguided?
