# Record of Resistance 02 — idea.quest editing boundary

## What AI suggested

The public workshop would remain a read-only snapshot. Project edits would happen in the local Windows Vault and appear online only after a separate republish.

## Why I rejected or revised it

When asked whether he accepted that boundary, Vihaan answered “no,” then clarified: “i wanna edit it directly from either obsidian claudian or chtgpt desktop app.” This records his desired editing surfaces, not a claim that automatic publishing, direct web editing, or a particular sync architecture has been approved.

## What I directed instead

Treat Claudian in Obsidian and the ChatGPT desktop app as ways to direct edits to the same project data. When offered a choice, Vihaan selected **automatic publication after every validated edit**, rather than a separate preview/approval publication action. Preserve the existing public site and private Vault boundary while that route is assessed; this preference is not a blanket approval to deploy all Vault changes.
